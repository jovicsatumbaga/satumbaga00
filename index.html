SPOTS AND VIEW



The Magnificent Balsa
Floating bamboo walkway/raft anchored in the water, giving visitors a stable spot to sit, rest, or jump off while swimming.

The Cliffside Bamboo Staircase
A hand-built staircase with bamboo steps, wooden plank reinforcements, and bamboo handrails designed for visitors to navigate the steep descent.

Underwater Snorkeling
The clear, deep blue-green water showcases the natural marine environment preserved in marine sanctuaries.

Secluded Cove Beach
Peaceful shoreline where white sand and coral pebbles meet the calm ocean waves.


About This Sanctuary

Kuliatan Marine Sanctuary: San Joaquin's Hidden Coastal Paradise — Philippines Along the southern tip of Iloilo province, where coastal hills meet the deep blue waters of the Panay Gulf, lies a coastal haven that stands as a shimmering testament to community-led marine conservation. Kuliatan Marine Sanctuary in Barangay Sinugbuhan offers travelers an immersive escape into biodiversity, rustic tranquility, and pristine aquatic life.


A Thriving Marine World

For generations, the surrounding marine waters served as primary sustenance for local fisherfolk.
